## Classification rules

- `Owner-confirmed` records the project owner's explicit provenance statement.
- `Third-party mark` means CARZON does not claim ownership and uses the mark only
  to identify the relevant brand or action.
- `UNKNOWN` or `UNRESOLVED` means no source record was found; no ownership or
  license claim should be made until evidence is recorded.
